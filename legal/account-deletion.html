Ludi - Account Deletion Request

If you wish to delete your Ludi account and associated data, you can do so in two ways:

Option 1: Delete from within the App

1 Open the Ludi app on your device.
2 Go to Settings (the "More" tab).
3 Scroll to the bottom to Account Management section.
4 Tap Delete Account.
5 Follow the confirmation prompts and type "DELETE" to confirm.

Option 2: Request via Email

Send an email to ludi.gameandteach@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Ludi account.

Our team will process your request within 5 business days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

When you delete your Ludi account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account credentials - Your email address and authentication records (Firebase Auth)
  • User profile - Your nickname, avatar, and display name
  • Game progress - XP, level, streak, hearts, and lesson completion records
  • Practice data - Weakness tracking and practice history
  • Leaderboard entry - Your ranking will be removed from the leaderboard

Data retention: All associated data is deleted immediately upon account deletion. No data is retained beyond the deletion process.

Data That Will Not Be Deleted

The following data is not linked to your account and will not be affected:

  • Anonymous usage statistics - Aggregated, non-identifiable analytics data

Subscription Cancellation

Please note that deleting your Ludi account does not automatically cancel your active Ludi Pro subscription. To cancel your subscription, please cancel it through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store before deleting your account.
